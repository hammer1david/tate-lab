Weekly rules are simulator inputs for now. Later TWETE can supply the same constraints automatically from the athlete's week cards and profile.

Checked weekdays are available training days. Unchecked weekdays are unavailable and can never receive a session. Checked days can be assigned as Easy, Workout Day, or Long Run Day.

Training phase placeholders remain Loading, Base, Sharpening and Tapering. Phase-specific training rules are still intentionally inactive.
